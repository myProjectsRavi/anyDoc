## Evidence and scope
`PdfCompareTool.compare` calls `pdfDoc.writeTo(it)` with a plain `FileOutputStream(stagedFile)` after rendering all pages. The staged publisher checks coroutine cancellation after the writer completes and before publication, but PDF serialization has no intra-write cancellation checks. Large output writes can delay cancellation even though staging prevents cancelled output publication. Source-level risk only; no Android-device reproduction.

Implement a minimal JVM-testable OutputStream adapter that checks cancellation before each bounded <=64 KiB byte chunk and each single-byte write. Keep stream ownership with the caller; preserve bytes, IO exceptions, cancellation propagation, and staged output semantics. Add deterministic JVM regressions for bytes/chunks/cancellation/bounds/failures. Validate exact code/test SHA through draft-PR CI before closure.
