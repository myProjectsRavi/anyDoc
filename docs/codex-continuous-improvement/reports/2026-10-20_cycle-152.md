## Source evidence
`PdfCompressor.compressWithJpegRasterization` invokes `onProgress?.invoke(i + 1, renderer.pageCount)` after page rendering, JPEG encoding and content drawing without a cancellation check directly before callback dispatch. Cancellation during those operations can still dispatch a stale progress event before the next loop or save check. P2 callback responsiveness, not a reproduced crash.

## Planned acceptance
Introduce minimal preflight immediately before callback; preserve success callbacks, cleanup, exceptions and staged output. Add focused JVM tests for cancellation, ordering, multipage and coroutine behavior. Require exact-SHA nine-gate draft-PR CI. No Android device, emulator, benchmark or race-freedom claims.
