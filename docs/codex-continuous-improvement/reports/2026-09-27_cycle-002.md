## Why this cycle exists

The foreground batch service could request sticky restart while the executable queue lives only in process-local memory. After process death, Android can recreate a sticky service without the original task-id intent or runtime queue state. Until durable queue recovery is implemented, that restart policy is unsafe.

## Story US-R002-P1-01A

- Replace sticky restart behavior for active/running batch execution with fail-safe non-sticky behavior.
- Lock the policy with a regression test.
- Require normal branch CI before completion.
