## Source evidence
`DocForgeApp.onTrimMemory()` schedules `cleanStaleTempFiles(maxAgeMs = 0L)`; the cleanup filters by `docforge_` prefix, modification time and `ActiveTempFileRegistry.isActive(file)`. The active registry is opt-in and does not guard the interval before registration. Newly created in-flight cache files may be deleted under memory pressure.
